5. How We Protect Your Data

  • This website does not store your booking details. When you press the booking button, your details go straight into a WhatsApp message to us.

  • Booking messages are kept on our business WhatsApp and are accessible only to the owner and staff who need them to carry out bookings.
  • We reasonably safeguard this data against loss, misuse and unauthorised access. If a personal data breach ever affects you, we will inform you and the authorities as required by law.
